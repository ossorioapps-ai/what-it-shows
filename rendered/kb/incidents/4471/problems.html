1 · parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so 07/04 (7 April) becomes 4 July. The bodies show a clean swap: the day and the month trade places. These come in by app, email and the booking-v2 form, from every tenant.

Fix: parse with the tenant’s locale, not a fixed month-first order.

2 · hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People follow the hint and type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as parser, but the parser is not involved. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant are this problem; the same tenants’ app and email reports are parser.

Fix: print the hint from the tenant’s locale (DD/MM/YYYY for every tenant here). The parser fix does not change this.

3 · one-day: dates move by one day, not by a swap

These reports move the date by one day or depend on where the reader is: “Tuesday 12 May” in the invite, Monday 11 May in the calendar; “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday”; “every booking in June is one day off”. No month/day swap explains a one-day move. The desk had already read them this way (desk notes: “read as a one-day move”) but filed them under parser by tenant.

Fix: look at how the invite turns a booking time into a calendar date across time zones (midnight and all-day bookings, daylight saving). The parser fix does not change this.
